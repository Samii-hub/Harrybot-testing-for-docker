# HarryBot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 6, 2026

## 1. Overview

HarryBot is a small, privately operated Discord bot created for personal use.

This Privacy Policy explains what information HarryBot accesses and how that information is used.

## 2. Information HarryBot Accesses

HarryBot may receive information made available through the Discord API when messages are sent in a Discord server where the bot is present.

This can include:

- Message content
- The identity of the Discord user who sent the message
- The Discord channel where the message was sent

HarryBot uses this information only to determine whether a message was sent by the configured user and whether the bot should respond.

## 3. Information Storage

HarryBot does not intentionally store:

- Discord messages
- Message history
- User profiles
- User IDs
- Personal information

Message information is processed while the bot is running and is not saved to a database or other persistent storage by HarryBot.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information received through Discord is used only to provide HarryBot's intended functionality.

For example, HarryBot may check who sent a message and respond with a predefined message when the configured user speaks.

The information is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or marketing.

## 5. Information Sharing

HarryBot does not sell, rent, or share user information with third parties.

Discord itself may process information according to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

## 6. Data Deletion

Because HarryBot does not intentionally retain personal user data, there is normally no stored HarryBot data to delete.

If HarryBot's functionality changes in the future to store user information, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 7. Security

Reasonable steps are taken to keep the bot's credentials, including its Discord bot token, private and secure.

However, no software or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if HarryBot's functionality or handling of information changes.

The latest version will be published at the same location as this document.

## 9. Contact

Questions or concerns about HarryBot's privacy practices can be directed to the bot operator through Discord.

## 10. Discord

HarryBot is independently operated and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Discord Inc.

Use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
